# Fidelity and completeness review

Review the transcript and draft directly, segment by segment. Maintain working correspondence notes rather than relying on memory or a general impression. These notes are working artifacts, not additions to the speaker's article.

## Source to article

For each sequential source segment, locate the corresponding draft passages and account for:

- Claims, facts, numerical values, units, dates, names, and technical terminology.
- Arguments and intermediate reasoning, causal links, assumptions, supporting evidence, objections, concessions, and responses.
- Every substantive example, analogy, comparison, procedure, and ordered step.
- Qualifications, exceptions, uncertainty, opinions, attribution, and conclusions.

Mark omissions and altered meanings, then repair the draft from the source. Record removal reasons for potentially meaningful passages: filler, unrelated promotion, accidental repetition, or genuine redundancy without loss of argumentative function. Retain uncertain cases. Meaningful repetition is not disposable merely because the conclusion was already stated.

## Article to source

For each substantive draft statement, find its support in the transcript. Remove unsupported facts, inferred explanations, new causal links, invented transitions in reasoning, and conclusions stronger than the speaker's. Metadata can disambiguate terms but cannot supply additional article claims. External verification belongs only in the separate critique.

Check corrections against their confidence and preserve unresolved ambiguity. Check that perspective, speaker attribution, certainty, and distinctions between hypotheses and established facts have survived. Suspected speaker errors and contradictions must remain in the article.

## Focused ambiguity checks

For passages containing unclear references, corrections, asides, or unlabelled dialogue, compare the source and draft explicitly:

- **Relationships and attribution:** Verify who did, said, or experienced what, and how people, places, and events are related. Distinguish a speaker's correction from a new assertion. Check the evidence for added names and speaker switches; timestamps and headings alone supply none. Preserve unresolved relationships rather than completing a plausible story.
- **Asides and reported speech:** Distinguish quoted opinions, audience questions, interruptions, and recording remarks from the speaker's substantive claims. An aside may be removed when irrelevant, but its removal or integration must not create an additional person, object, action, or claim.
- **Certainty and quantities:** Compare qualifiers, negation, frequency, numerical values, units, and time expressions in context. Preserve whether a time denotes a start, end, duration, or range; keep an unclear relation uncertain even when a number is legible. Preserve the strength of statements, especially where a later objection responds to it.
- **Publication and source details:** Account for substantive mentions of where material was published, shared, or can be found, including channels, platforms, links, and references. Separate useful provenance from removable promotion rather than dropping both together.

For any repair in these passages, recheck the changed wording against the corresponding source and its neighboring context. Keep the original claim intact where uncertainty remains; record the uncertainty separately instead of silently resolving it.

## Whole article

Read sequentially after assembly, returning to source ranges as needed:

- Topic order, reasoning order, procedural sequence, and meaningful original headings remain intact.
- Segment boundaries have neither dropped nor duplicated content; references and unfinished thoughts continue correctly.
- Paragraphs and headings reflect topic boundaries rather than artificial segment limits.
- Both editing modes preserve equivalent substantive content; style changes fit the selected mode.
- Available section timestamps, visual dependencies, significant ambiguities, and source metadata are represented according to configuration.
- Notes and critique are separated from the speaker's prose; Markdown is well formed and the original file remains unchanged.

Finish only after identified fidelity discrepancies are repaired or unresolved source ambiguities are explicitly flagged. Record the source ranges reviewed and outstanding issues in working notes. Do not label unchecked ranges as reviewed. If comparing modes, compare their substantive coverage directly, rather than judging which sounds better. No automatic check or independent reviewer guarantees complete detection.
